Motivation and Benefits
Financial institutions face increasing challenges in keeping up with rapidly evolving
regulatory requirements issued by authorities such as MAS, FATF, FinCEN, ECB and
other governing bodies. Manual tracking of regulatory changes is time-consuming,
prone to oversight, and often leads to delays in updating internal compliance
processes.
This project proposes the development of an automated regulatory monitoring and
compliance management system that integrates regulatory sources, detects changes,
assesses their impact, and supports compliance teams with timely alerts, workflow
tools, and audit-ready documentation.
Details
Project Objectives
• Automate the retrieval of regulatory updates from multiple international and local
regulatory bodies.
• Detect and analyze changes in regulatory documents and advisories.
• Assess how new regulations affect existing compliance processes.
• Provide automated alerts and user-friendly dashboards for compliance teams.
• Maintain a centralized, searchable archive of changes and corresponding actions for
audit purposes.
Proposed System Features
1. Regulatory Feed Integration
The system will connect to external regulatory sources—including government portals,
central banks, and industry bodies—through APIs or web scraping. It will automatically
retrieve updates from organizations such as FATF, FinCEN, MAS, ECB, and FCA, and
store parsed information in an internal knowledge base.
2. Change Detection
The system will scan regulatory documents, consultation papers, announcements, and
advisories at regular intervals (approximately every two weeks). It will detect
modifications by comparing new versions with historical documents and highlight
specific differences.
3. Impact Assessment
Each detected regulatory update will undergo an automated impact analysis. The
system will determine which compliance areas, internal controls, or customer-facing
processes are affected. High-impact or high-risk changes—such as those associated
with penalties—will be prioritized.
4. Notification and Alert System
Compliance teams will receive automated notifications when significant regulatory
changes occur. Alerts will be categorized by severity, such as:
• Immediate Action Required
• Review Recommended
• Informational
5. Knowledge Base Update
The system will help update internal compliance documentation, including KYC/AML
procedures, operational guidelines, and training materials. It will also maintain version
history to track how procedures evolve over time.
6. Compliance Gap Analysis
By comparing existing bank policies against new regulatory requirements, the system
will identify areas where procedures are outdated or non-compliant. These gaps will be
flagged for remediation.
7. Task and Workflow Management
For each identified regulatory change, the system will generate tasks for relevant
departments to review, update, and implement necessary changes. Workflow features
include:
• Task assignment
• Deadline setting
• Progress tracking
8. Historical Archive and Audit Trail
The platform will maintain a fully searchable archive of all regulatory changes and
actions taken in response. This supports internal audits and provides evidence for
external regulatory inspections.
9. Reporting and Dashboards
The system will generate summary reports categorizing regulatory changes (e.g., AML,
data privacy, consumer protection). Management dashboards will display outstanding
compliance tasks, progress indicators, and trend insights.
